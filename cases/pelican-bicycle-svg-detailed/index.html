The rider

Brown pelican proportions: the gular pouch runs the full length of the mandible, the nape is butter-cream, and the wing's secondaries fold in five overlapping tiers. Webbed feet stay on the pedals through a full crank revolution.

The machine

Double-butted diamond frame, 32 spokes per wheel laced in two crosses, 44-tooth chainring driving a 17-tooth cog, drop bars wrapped in cork, and a brass bell that actually sits on the bar.

The coast

Parallax dune grass, sea oats, a pier fading into haze and a rolling break. Sun position drives the sky stops, the water sheen and the length of the cast shadow together.
