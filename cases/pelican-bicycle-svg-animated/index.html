The rider

Pelecanus velocipedus. Rides upright, steers with the near wing, keeps the evening catch in the pouch. The scarf reads the breeze; the crest and pouch lag a half-beat behind the saddle so the bounce never looks rigid.

The shoreline

Seven parallax bands move at their own rate — clouds, headland, sails, swell, palms, railing, planks — so distance reads without a single raster pixel. Sand sprays off the rear contact patch and settles behind the rider.
