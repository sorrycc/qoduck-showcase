02 — the shed

A boat shed that smells of peach and jasmine

Mara Quint started Low Tide in 2019, in a former boat repair shed by the harbor. The doors still open the way boat doors do — wide, and straight onto the quay.

Forty kilos a week goes through a 1962 Probat drum roaster. It is older than most of the equipment in this town and it does one thing well: it takes its time.

Light roasts only. Nothing is taken dark to hide anything, so the origin stays in the cup — peach and jasmine from Guji, red apple and panela from Huila, blackcurrant from Nyeri.

Tuesday and Monday the shop is shut and the drum is running. Wednesday the doors open at eight, and by Sunday the shelf is usually bare.
