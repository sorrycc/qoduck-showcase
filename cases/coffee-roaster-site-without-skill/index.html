The shed

A boat shed that smells of peach on a Tuesday.

Mara Quint started Low Tide in 2019, in a former boat repair shed by the harbor. It still feels like a boat shed, which is the point.

We roast 40 kg a week on a 1962 Probat drum roaster. Hand wheel, old drum, no interest in being hurried.

Light roasts only. Monday and Tuesday the shop stays shut so the drum can run. By Wednesday the bags are on the shelf, and by Sunday they are usually gone.
